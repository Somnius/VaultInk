Fixes for two things kos25k reported after 0.77.0.

Checked on my X4 Pro (Fira Sans 16 pt):
- Words no longer glue together with Space condensing and Optical spacing on (#30). A word gap never goes under 60% of a space.
- Letter spacing has five steps: Off, Small, Medium, Large, Max. On a loose line the letters take the spare room first.
- A swipe from the top edge opens quick settings over an open book menu, no tap first (#15).

Tests on a computer only:
- A very loose line can take one or two words from the line above (Hyphenation on).
- The swipe over the classic list menu; no swipe while a popup is up.

Known: switching fonts can change the font size. The GitHub certificate check is still off in release builds.
